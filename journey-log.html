Purpose: electronic filling and formatting of the issued Journey Log only. This tool does not replace the approved OFP, SOP, current flight-information sources or airline-approved EFB applications.

Only Air Astana Journey Logs are supported.

The form is drawn from the document you load — its legs, its crew, its number of rows. Nothing leaves the device.

The return key steps to the next box. Times take four digits — 0340 becomes 03:40. Blk and Flt are worked out from the times either side until you write in them yourself.
